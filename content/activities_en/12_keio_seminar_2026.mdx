On October 30, 2026, I was invited by **Prof. Shingo Murata** to give a seminar talk on **“PredVLA: Predictive Sensorimotor Modeling for Sub-Million-Parameter Robot Manipulation”** at his lab in the Department of Electronics and Electrical Engineering, Faculty of Science and Technology, Keio University.

<br />

In the talk, I introduced PredVLA's core idea of building a small robot controller around predicting sensorimotor dynamics and inferring its latent state from prediction error, rather than compressing it as an observation-to-action mapping, along with its results on the LIBERO benchmark.

<br />

The discussion gave me interesting perspectives on how PredVLA could be extended, along with feedback from researchers who specialize in VLA models, which I learned a great deal from.

<br />

I am grateful to Prof. Murata for the invitation and to the members of the lab.
